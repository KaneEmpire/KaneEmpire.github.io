About This Game

Hello and welcome to the most law-abiding corner of cyberspace!! In every other game set in a sunny city, you steal the cars. In ATG −VI you drive the tow truck that brings them back. Without a scratch, please. The story starts at the end and runs backwards.

What Is Inside

  • 6 cases told in reverse, from tonight back to one year ago
  • Real 3D graphics (very advanced)
  • Miami: flamingos, lifeguard towers, neon, one cruise ship
  • An epilogue where you close nightclubs on foot
  • FLOOR 55: CLASSIFIED. Somebody up there keeps phoning.
UNDER CONSTRUCTION: FLOOR 55 FILE

How To Play

Phone: hold to drive, drag sideways to steer, pull down to brake.
Computer: arrow keys or W A S D.
Less chaos = more pay. It is that simple!
